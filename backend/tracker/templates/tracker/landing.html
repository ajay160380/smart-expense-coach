Common Questions

Everything you need to know before connecting your WhatsApp number.

How does the WhatsApp AI categorization work? +
Send casual messages in English, Hindi, or Hinglish like "150 for coffee" or "250 ka petrol dalwaya". ExpenseTracker automatically extracts the amount, category, and whether it was Necessary or Discretionary in ~840ms with zero dropdown forms.
Can I send WhatsApp voice notes instead of typing? +
Yes. Hold the mic in WhatsApp and speak naturally — for example "50 rupay chai aur 120 auto". Our audio pipeline transcribes the voice note, logs the spend, and replies immediately with your remaining daily balance.
How does Safe-to-Spend Today & AI Coach budgeting work? +
Once you set a monthly budget target, ExpenseTracker divides your remaining allowance across the days left in your cycle. If spending spikes on one day, your daily target recalibrates automatically, with anomaly alerts, streaks, and optional AI Coach roasts.
How do Group & Trip Splits work for friends? +
Create a split for hostel dinners, cabs, or trips like Goa directly from the Web Dashboard or Android app. ExpenseTracker computes exact net balances and gives you a live shareable link that friends can open in any browser without signing up.
Do I need to install a separate app? +
No. WhatsApp alone handles daily logging and instant budget replies. The Web Analytics Dashboard and native Android APK stay synced in real time whenever you want deep charts, notepad goals, or trip split management.
Can I edit an expense or track personal savings goals? +
Yes. Every transaction logged via WhatsApp can be edited or deleted anytime from the Web Dashboard or Android app, and your budget recalculates immediately. You can also manage quick notes and savings goals inside the Notepad & Goals tab.
Can I export my expenses or delete my account anytime? +
Yes — there is zero lock-in. Export your transaction ledger cleanly as CSV or JSON from the dashboard, or permanently delete your account and stored data anytime from the Delete Account page.
Is my financial data private and encrypted? +
Yes. All expenses are transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely strictly for your personal budgeting, alerts, and reports — never sold to third parties or advertisers.
