OUR STORY

Making finance effortless

ExpenseTracker was built because we were tired of bloated apps. Tracking expenses should be as simple as sending a WhatsApp message to a friend.

Our Mission

Eliminate the friction of personal finance. People want to track money, but habits break when opening an app for a ₹50 chai feels tedious. Bringing your expense ledger into WhatsApp keeps daily awareness at 100%.

The Aha Moment

Why force another app install when billions already open WhatsApp every hour?

AI Powered

LLMs decode natural English, Hindi, and Hinglish messages deterministically with zero dropdown forms.

Privacy First

Your ledger is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell transaction histories to advertisers or third-party brokers.

How We Got Here

EARLY 2026

The Friction Problem

After deleting manual budget apps every two weeks, we saw the problem wasn't discipline — it was opening slow multi-screen forms for tiny everyday spends.

MID 2026

The WhatsApp + LLM Prototype

A quick Hinglish parser inside WhatsApp evolved into real-time safe-to-spend targets, voice note transcription, and split trip links.

TODAY

ExpenseTracker Platform

Serving 10,000+ daily spenders seamlessly synced across WhatsApp, Web Analytics Dashboard, and native Android APK.
